## Who writes this

The Bow Bench is written by K. Adem Cooper.

I am new to archery. My family took it up, I came along, and I shoot barebow, which is the recurve division without a sight, a clicker or stabilisers.

## What being new means for what you read here

I am not going to tell you I have shot for twenty years. I have not, and you would find out.

So this site does not ask you to trust my judgement. It asks you to check my working. That is a different promise, and it is one I can actually keep.

Every number on a comparison page is marked as one of two things. **Measured** means it was taken here, with the method written on the page. **Maker** means the manufacturer published it and nobody here has checked it. Prices say which shop they came from and the day they were read.

Where a number is not known, the table shows a dash. Where a rule is quoted, the article number is next to it and the rulebook is linked at the end of the page.

A page that says "nothing here was measured" is telling you something useful. Most gear sites will not tell you that.

## Where I do have background

Trap shooting, for a while now. It is a different sport, but it teaches the same habits that matter here: equipment that is set up the same way every time, range safety as a default, and a healthy suspicion of gear that promises to fix technique.

Outside the shooting sports it is fishing, hiking, and road and mountain biking. Enough years of buying outdoor equipment to know how much of a spec sheet is marketing.

None of that makes me a barebow coach. It does mean I know what an honest gear review looks like, because I have wanted one and not found it.

## Why this site exists

When my family started, I went looking for the barebow equivalent of what I would want before buying a bike or a shotgun. A table of real numbers. A clear statement of what the rules allow. Somebody willing to say a cheap item is good enough.

I did not find it. Barebow gear writing is mostly rewritten manufacturer copy, and a lot of it is wrong about the rules. Two errors I have already had to correct on this site were repeated across the first page of search results.

So the site is the reference I wanted, built as I learn, with the working shown.

## What I will not do

- Invent a measurement, a price or a specification.
- Take payment for a recommendation. See the [disclosure page](/disclosure/) for how the site earns.
- Write about hunting, compound bows or crossbows. Different sport, different site. <!-- allow-scope-terms -->
- Claim to have tested equipment I have only read about.

## Tell me when I am wrong

I will get things wrong, and being new means I will get more things wrong than most.

Rules change, prices move, and specifications are sometimes published incorrectly by the makers themselves. If something here is out of date or simply mistaken, [tell me](/contact/) and I will correct it and re-date the page.

Corrections are logged rather than quietly edited, because a site that claims to be careful should show the times it was not.
