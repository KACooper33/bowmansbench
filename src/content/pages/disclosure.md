## How The Bow Bench makes money

The Bow Bench earns a commission when a reader buys through some of the links on this site.

The price you pay is the same either way. A commission is paid by the retailer out of the sale, not added to your total.

Not every link earns a commission. Where the best information or the best price sits with a retailer that has no affiliate programme, the link goes there anyway and earns nothing.

## What earning a commission does not change

A commission never buys a recommendation. No manufacturer or retailer pays to appear on this site, and none is given a preview of a page before it publishes.

Where a product is a poor choice, the page says so, including when that product carries a higher commission than the one it loses to. Every comparison page names one overall pick, one budget pick and one premium pick, and those picks are made before the affiliate links are added.

The gear makers written about most often on this site have no affiliate programmes at all. That means the site earns nothing from many of the products it recommends most highly.

## How the numbers on this site are produced

Every number on a comparison page is marked as one of two things.

**Measured** means the number was taken on our own bench, with the method described on the page. The page states what was weighed, what tool was used, and what was not tested.

**Maker** means the number was published by the manufacturer and has not been independently checked.

No number on this site is estimated, inferred or copied from another site. Where a figure is not known, the table shows a dash rather than a guess.

## Why this page exists

United States law requires a clear and conspicuous disclosure of a material connection between a publisher and the products it recommends. An affiliate commission is such a connection.

This page sets out that connection in one place. A short disclosure also appears at the top of every page that carries affiliate links, rather than being buried in a footer.
