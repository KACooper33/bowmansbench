## Corrections come first

Corrections are the most useful thing you can send, and they get answered before anything else.

If you send one, the page is checked against the primary source, corrected if it is wrong, and re-dated. Two errors on this site have already been found and fixed that way, both of them rules that most gear sites get wrong.

It helps if you can say which page, which claim, and what the correct answer is. A rulebook article number or a link to the maker's own specification is ideal, but a rough pointer is far better than staying quiet.

## Makers and retailers

If a specification here is wrong, tell me and it will be corrected.

If you would like a product considered, say so, and say plainly whether you are offering to send one. Anything received is marked on the page as supplied for review, and it buys no verdict. See the [disclosure page](/disclosure/) for how the site earns and what that does and does not change.

Product photographs are welcome if you hold the rights and are granting permission to publish them. Every photograph on the site records where the right to use it came from.

## What I cannot help with

I am new to archery and this site is a gear reference, not a coaching service.

I cannot tell you what draw weight suits you, diagnose your form, or value a second-hand bow. A coach at your club will answer all three better in ten minutes than I could in an email.

Questions about what the rules allow are fair game, and if the answer is not already on the site it probably should be.

## Response times

This is written alongside a job, so a reply may take a week. Corrections are answered faster than anything else.
