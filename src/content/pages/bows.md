A barebow setup is four separate purchases that have to work together: a riser, a pair of limbs, a plunger, and a tab. Some retailers sell them as one package. Buying each part alone usually costs less and always lets you choose the parts yourself.

Start with the riser and the limbs, since those two decide draw weight and mass weight, the two numbers everything else is tuned around. The [riser hub](/risers/) and [limb hub](/limbs/) both compare current models on their published specifications.

If a poundage or a division question is holding up a purchase, the [start here guide](/start-here/) and the [barebow rules guide](/guides/barebow-rules/) cover what World Archery and the NFAA actually require, rather than what a retailer's marketing copy implies.
